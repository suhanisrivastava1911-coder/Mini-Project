Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our data sources, privacy, and accuracy

Does AI Scheme Sahayak submit applications on my behalf?

No. We provide intelligent matching, document readiness checklists, and direct verified links to official government portals (such as Udyam, JanSamarth, or PMEGP e-Portal). Final applications and approvals occur strictly on designated government portals.

Is my personal and business data secure?

Yes. Your privacy is paramount. We use PBKDF2 password hashing, AES-256 encryption for sensitive details (income, caste, district), and strict user consent protocols. You can also explore schemes anonymously or delete your profile anytime.

Can I use voice input in Hindi or my regional language?

Yes! In supported modern browsers, you can speak directly in Hindi or English using the microphone button on our AI Assistant page. Our NLP engine normalizes spoken Hinglish terms and conversational amounts (e.g., "50 hazar", "2 lakh").

How are demonstration records handled?

All officially verified government schemes are clearly tagged with an official ministry verification badge. Any synthetic or test records are prominently labelled as "DEMO / TEST SCHEME" to uphold absolute transparency.

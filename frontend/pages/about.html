AI-Driven Scheme Matching for Marginalized Entrepreneurs

A technology platform designed to bridge information asymmetry, language literacy barriers, and bureaucratic complexity for India's underserved micro and small business owners.

1. The Core Challenge

While the Government of India and various state authorities host over 3,000 welfare and enterprise development programs (such as PMEGP, Stand-Up India, PM SVANidhi, and PM Vishwakarma), marginalized entrepreneurs (SC/ST, Women, OBC, Rural Artisans, Street Vendors) struggle to discover and access them due to:

🗣️ Language Barriers

Portals and official gazettes are published in administrative English or formal Hindi, excluding non-fluent or vernacular-speaking artisans.

📐 Complex Eligibility Logic

Multi-tiered rules combine caste classification, rural vs urban subsidy rates, project cost limits, and bank collateral prerequisites.

📋 Paperwork Ambiguity

Entrepreneurs abandon viable applications due to uncertainty about what certificates, DPRs, and documents are strictly required.

2. Technology Architecture

Frontend

Interactive Web Application

HTML5, Modern Responsive CSS3, Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript, Progressive Web App (PWA) offline service caching, and Web Speech API.

Backend

Intelligent Python Engine

Python 3.12 + Flask RESTful architecture, modular NLP entity extraction, Hinglish text normalization, and 4-stage deterministic matching engine.

Data & Security

Resilient Relational Storage

MySQL 8.0 relational schema with local SQLite failover, PBKDF2-SHA256 password hashing, and AES-256 encryption for demographic data.

3. Transparency & Safeguards

Hard Gates & Zero Hallucination Guarantee

Unlike unstructured generative LLM chatbots that hallucinate non-existent government grants, our system enforces a deterministic two-tier logic:

1. Deterministic Hard Filter Gate: If an entrepreneur does not fulfill mandatory legal conditions (for example, Stand-Up India requires an SC/ST or Woman beneficiary, or PMEGP ceiling restrictions), the scheme is disqualified immediately. It is NEVER presented as officially eligible.
2. Explainable Recommendations: For every matched scheme, users see exact mathematical scoring reasons (e.g., "Passed mandatory gender gate", "Rural location qualifies for maximum 35% subsidy").
3. Verified vs Demo Badging: Official schemes verified against current ministry guidelines bear an official ministry tag. Synthetic or demonstration records are visibly tagged as DEMO RECORD.

4. Privacy & Data Ethics

We uphold the highest ethical standards regarding personal and business data:

  • Explicit User Consent: Personal and demographic details are only stored when the user checks the explicit consent box.
  • Anonymous Browsing: Any entrepreneur can explore public schemes without creating an account or logging in.
  • Data Deletion: Users have the right to inspect, download (JSON format), or permanently delete their profiles at any time.
  • Zero Commercial Monetization: User data is never sold, leased, or transmitted to third-party commercial marketing brokers.

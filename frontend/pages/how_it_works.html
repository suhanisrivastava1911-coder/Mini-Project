The 4-Stage Intelligent Matching Engine

How AI Scheme Sahayak eliminates guesswork and connects marginalized entrepreneurs to verified government capital

1

Multilingual NLP & Speech Input

Entrepreneurs describe their business, location, caste, and loan requirements in English, Hindi, Hinglish, or their regional language using speech or conversational text.

Example: Spoken input "Hamari handloom ki dukan hai Varanasi mein, 3 lakh loan chahiye" is processed through Hinglish entity normalizers to extract: 
{ "business_sector": "Textiles & Handloom", "district": "Varanasi", "state": "Uttar Pradesh", "funding_requirement": 300000 }
2

Deterministic Hard Eligibility Filter Gate

Before calculating relevance scores, the engine runs strict mandatory eligibility rules:

Target Gender: Non-women entrepreneurs cannot pass women-only schemes (e.g. Stand-Up India for non-SC/ST).
Caste / Social Category: Strict SC/ST criteria for specialized concession schemes (e.g. NSFDC MSY).
State / Jurisdiction: State-specific schemes (e.g. UP MMYSY) are restricted to entrepreneurs in that state.
Financial Caps: Maximum project costs and turnover ceilings strictly enforced.
3

Weighted Relevance Scoring (0 - 100)

Schemes that pass the hard gate receive a transparent multi-factor score:

Funding Alignment: Match between requested capital and scheme benefit ceiling.
Sector Fit: Priority match for targeted crafts, manufacturing, or services.
Social Category Priority: Higher weighting for marginalized communities.
Stage Suitability: Greenfield ideation vs early expansion fit.
4

Actionable Guidance & Official Portals

Entrepreneurs receive ranked recommendations with clear reasoning, required document checklists, and direct links to official government application portals.
